# HabitTracker

A small practice project.

## Features
Available now:
- Track points for your day.
- Saving your daily score between browser sessions.

Planned features:
- Get a glance of how you did on previous days
- See your overall statistics

## Motivation

The app is designed to solve a real-world problem that a lot of us, including me, might have problems with. 
Many people try to quit bad habits. Naturally, you may slip up and do the bad habit. Usually, the mind goes like: *"oh no, my day is ruined!"*, and then you let that one slip-up take over the rest of your day, thinking *"Well, I've done it once, might do it again, what's left to lose?"*

**That's not true!**

This small app is aimed to remind you, that whenever you unexpectedly do a bad habit, you still have a lot of good habits up your sleeve. It could be routine things: doing the dishes, cleaning the room, making your bed; or it could be something more specific, like working out, doing your favorite hobbies, getting up at specific time, etc.

Your day can be thought of as a number of points. When you do a good habit, you gain a point, and when you do a bad habit, you lose a point.
